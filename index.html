Our Story

Born from a love
of extraordinary

Fifteen years ago, we planned our first wedding in a sun-drenched Tuscan villa — and never looked back. Maison Céleste was born not from a business plan, but from an obsession: the belief that a wedding should feel like a dream you never want to wake from.

We work with a deliberately small number of couples each year — because intimacy is everything. We listen before we suggest, understand before we create, and pour ourselves entirely into your singular love story.
